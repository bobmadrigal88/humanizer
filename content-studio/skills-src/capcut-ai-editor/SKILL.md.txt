# CapCut AI editor

Rename this file to `SKILL.md` after unzipping.

## Honesty note

The source video builds a full travel vlog with CapCut's AI tools, starting from
a consistent character. Its transcript could not be read from the cloud machine
(YouTube was blocked). The steps below rebuild its method from the video's own
description, CapCut's official guides and 2026 reviews. Menu names change between
versions; check them in the installed app.

## The 6-step method

| Step | CapCut tool | What to do | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Script | AI Writer, Script to Video | Draft the beats and voice-over | Treat the output as a draft. Rewrite the hook and last line yourself. Run the humanizer skill on it. |
| 2. Character | AI image, reference images | Make one anchor image. Write a fixed identity description. | CapCut says references do not guarantee the same face. Change only action, place and camera between prompts. |
| 3. Clips | AI video (image-to-video, Seedance 2.0 in some regions) | Animate each scene from the anchor still | Image-to-video drifts less than text-to-video. |
| 4. Voice | Text to speech, Enhance voice | One voice for the whole video | Keep the same voice for every line. Voice cloning is Pro. |
| 5. Assembly | Auto captions, Smart Cut, keyframes, AI music | Cut silences, add captions, slow zooms, music under the voice | Always proofread auto captions. |
| 6. Polish and export | Relight, Remove background, Auto reframe | Match light between clips, reframe 9:16 | Export one master, then one cut per platform. |

## Feature cheat sheet (desktop)

| Feature | Where | Free or Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Auto captions | Left toolbar: Captions, then Auto captions, then Generate | Free (limits) |
| Text to speech | Text layer, then Text to speech in the right panel | Free (limits) |
| Enhance voice | Select clip, then Audio, then Enhance voice | Check plan |
| Smart Cut / AutoCut | Toolbar | Limited free |
| Remove background | Select clip, then Video, then Remove BG | Free (limits) |
| Relight | Adjust panel | Check plan |
| Auto reframe | Video, then Basic, then Auto reframe | Pro in some regions |
| Keyframes | Diamond icon next to any property | Free |
| 4K export, voice clone, vocal isolation | Export and Audio panels | Pro |

## Editing rules that make it look pro

1. **Hook in 2 seconds.** Start on the most interesting frame, not a greeting.
2. **Cut every 2 to 4 seconds** on talking shots. Use a jump cut or a B-roll cut
   on each new idea.
3. **Cut on action.** End a clip mid-movement and start the next mid-movement.
4. **B-roll on every "show me" line.** When the script says "look at", show it.
5. **Captions:** 1 to 3 words at a time, centered in the safe zone, high
   contrast, the key word in an accent color.
6. **Slow push-in** (keyframe scale 100% to 108%) on static talking shots.
7. **Sound:** voice at about -14 LUFS, music 18 to 24 dB under the voice, a soft
   whoosh or pop on big cuts only.
8. **One color look** across all clips. Relight clips that do not match.
9. **Safe zones (1080 x 1920):** keep text out of the top 270 px, the bottom
   670 px and the right 160 px.

## Free alternative (no CapCut)

Everything above also runs with free tools: ffmpeg for cuts and audio, Whisper
for captions, and the `assemble.py` tool in `content-studio/tools/`.
