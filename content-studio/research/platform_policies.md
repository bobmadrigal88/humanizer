# Platform rules for Valentina + Mito's first video (IG, FB, TikTok)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Video: 1:30 vertical talking-head clip. Valentina is a photoreal AI avatar made with Higgsfield. Mito is a real senior Beagle. The topic is 3 at-home mouth checks. The video ends with a mention of the Amazon affiliate product "Iron Paws".

**How this was researched:** Direct page fetches from facebook.com, tiktok.com and ftc.gov were blocked in this environment. Every point below comes from web search results, which quote official pages (Meta, TikTok, FTC, FDA, Amazon) and news sites (TechCrunch, Engadget, Social Media Today). Points backed only by marketing blogs, or by nothing at all, are marked **unverified**. Check those in the app before you rely on them.

---

## Main points

1. **Label the AI on every platform.** Valentina is a photoreal AI person. Meta and TikTok both expect a label. Since 31 Aug 2026, Instagram limits the reach of accounts that show an AI person and do not use the "AI-generated profile" label.
2. **Remove the Iron Paws mention from this first video.** Your course rule already says "no selling before 1,000 followers". The law also requires it: if you name a product you earn commission on, the FTC and Amazon both require a disclosure such as #ad. "No #ad" and "mention the affiliate product" cannot both apply to the same video.
3. **Valentina must never say she personally used a product.** She is not a real person, so a first-person testimonial from her is a fake testimonial under the 2024 FTC rule.
4. **Make no treatment claims.** Say nothing like "cures tartar", "kills bacteria" or "fixes gut problems". Tell viewers to see a vet.
5. **Keep to 5 hashtags or fewer** on Instagram and TikTok. Use 1 to 3 on Facebook.
6. **Use real questions, not bait.** "Comment YES" or "comment X for the link" is engagement bait on Meta. On TikTok, comment-to-DM automation is not available in the US, UK or EU.

**What to do on IG and FB:**
- Turn on the **AI-generated profile** label on the Instagram account before the first post.
- Turn on the **AI label** on each Reel (IG and FB).
- Put a line in the bio, such as "Valentina is an AI-generated host · Mito is a real senior Beagle 🐶".
- Keep Higgsfield's C2PA metadata in the file. Don't strip it on purpose. Meta may auto-label the video anyway.

## 3. "Comment KEYWORD for the link" CTAs

| Question | Answer | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is comment-to-DM automation (ManyChat) available on TikTok? | **Not for most accounts.** ManyChat's TikTok automations need Advanced Access or a Verified Business Account and are not offered in the EU, UK or Switzerland. Reports say the comment trigger is live only in a few Asian markets (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia). Viewers must **DM you a keyword first**. | Partly verified (ManyChat help + community + competitor) | https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/17961702404892-Use-cases-and-strategies-for-TikTok-automations-on-Manychat , https://community.manychat.com/general-q-a-43/tiktok-auto-dm-from-comments-6480 , https://instantdm.com/blog/tiktok-comment-to-dm |
| Comment-to-DM on Instagram? | Yes, through Meta's official API (ManyChat and others). Limits: **1 private reply per comment, within 7 days**, then a 24-hour messaging window. | Partly verified (vendor pages quoting Meta docs) | https://getkanal.com/blog/manychat-instagram , https://inro.social/blog/instagram-comment-to-dm-automation |
| Does Meta penalize engagement bait? | **Yes.** Meta defines it as posts that "explicitly ask for votes, shares, comments, tags, or likes" for no real purpose. It demotes those posts, and Pages that do it repeatedly get stronger demotions. **Real questions and requests for advice are allowed.** | Verified (Meta Transparency Center) | https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/ , https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/ |
| Is "comment X for the link" bait on Meta? | It asks for a comment to get a reward, so it can be read as bait. Meta has no specific rule on keyword CTAs. Many large accounts use them. The risk is low to moderate, but a new account has no trust history yet. | **Unverified** (judgment) | — |
| Does TikTok penalize engagement bait? | Engagement bait, such as "like for like", can make a video **ineligible for the For You feed**. A plain question to the audience is not listed as bait. | Partly verified | https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity , https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-algorithm-explained |
| TikTok bio link | Personal accounts reportedly need 1,000 followers for a clickable bio link. Business accounts may get one sooner. | **Unverified** for 2026 | — |

**CTAs that work for a new account with no links (all platforms):**
- A real question: "Which of the 3 checks did Mito's age surprise you on? Tell me your dog's age 👇". This is allowed because it asks for real information.
- "Save this for your next check." Saves are a strong signal and are not bait.
- "Follow for part 2: what the vet checks next." This builds a series.
- "Send this to someone with a senior dog." This asks people to share something useful. It is not a bare "share this".
- Avoid "Comment YES", "Tag 3 friends", "Like if you love dogs" and "Comment IRON for the link" until after 1,000 followers. When you start linking, use comment-to-DM on IG only. On TikTok, ask people to "DM me the word ___".

## 4. FTC, Amazon and pet-health claim rules

### 4a. AI personas and fake testimonials
| Rule | What it means here | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule** (final Aug 2024, effective 21 Oct 2024) | Bans creating or spreading reviews or testimonials **by someone who does not exist** (AI-generated included) or by someone without real experience with the product. Civil penalties can reach **about $53,088 per violation** (2025 figure). | Verified (FTC) | https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials , https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers , https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-warns-10-companies-about-possible-violations-agencys-new-consumer-review-rule |
| **FTC Endorsement Guides (2023)** | The definition of "endorsement" now covers **virtual influencers**. Endorsements must reflect the real experience and honest opinion of the endorser. | Verified (FTC) | https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/06/federal-trade-commission-announces-updated-advertising-guides-combat-deceptive-reviews-endorsements |
| What this means for Valentina | She can **teach** general facts ("vets look for tartar at the gum line"). She must **not** say "I give Mito Iron Paws and his breath got better". That is a testimonial from a person who does not exist. No rule bans AI avatars in marketing. | Verified rule; application is our reading | same |

### 4b. Affiliate disclosure
| Rule | Detail | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disclosure is required whenever there is a material connection | An affiliate commission is a material connection. The disclosure must be clear and conspicuous. "#ad" works. "Commissionable link", #comped and #hosted are not clear enough. On video, put the disclosure **in the video** (spoken and on screen), not only in the caption. The FTC says caption-only disclosure on TikTok is "very unlikely" to be clear. | Verified (FTC) | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking , https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers |
| Platform tools are not enough on their own | Platform labels do not replace your own disclosure. Responsibility stays with the influencer and the brand. | Verified (FTC) | same |
| Amazon Associates | You must show "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" (linked to your account, for example in the bio). You must also disclose **each time** you share a link, in posts, stories, DMs and link-in-bio pages. "#ad", "#paidlink" or "#CommissionsEarned" all work. Register the **full profile URL** of each social account. No link shorteners that hide the Amazon destination. No incentives for clicking. | Verified (Amazon Associates Central) | https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies , https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement , https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/participation/ |
| Amazon's "500 followers" eligibility | Commonly cited as "in most cases, at least 500" organic followers. Also note Amazon closes accounts that make no qualifying sales within 180 days. | **Unverified** (blogs only) | https://azonpress.com/key-amazon-affiliate-requirements/ |
| TikTok commercial toggle | TikTok counts affiliate-link and commission content as **branded content**. Turn on **Content disclosure and ads → Disclose commercial content → "Your brand"**. This shows a "Promotional content" label. Undisclosed commercial content may be removed from the For You feed. | Verified (TikTok) | https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en , https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-turn-on-the-commercial-content-disclosure-setting-in-tiktok |

**Conflict with the course rule:** "No #ad on growth videos" is safe **only if the video does not name or promote the affiliate product**. If Iron Paws is mentioned and the user is an Amazon Associate for it, disclosure is required, with or without a link. **Recommendation: cut the Iron Paws line from this video.** Save it for later posts that carry a proper #ad, the Amazon statement and the TikTok commercial toggle.

### 4c. Pet supplement health claims
| Rule | Detail | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disease claims make a product a "drug" | Under the FD&C Act, any product "intended for use in the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of disease" in animals is a drug. FDA does **not** recognize "pet supplements" as their own category. They are either food or drugs. | Verified (FDA) | https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/fdas-regulation-pet-food |
| Real enforcement | FDA warning letters have cited claims such as "may aid in preventing… periodontal disease in dogs". | Verified (FDA warning letters) | https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/holista-llc-dba-holistapet-699082-04072025 , https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/house-alchemy-llc-dba-cbd-dog-health-hamet-love-llc-dba-mycodog-701064-04072025 |
| FTC | Health claims in ads must be backed by competent and reliable evidence. This applies to affiliates who repeat the claims too. | General FTC law; no pet-specific 2026 page found | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing |

**Safe and unsafe wording:**
- ✅ "Tartar at the gum line, bad breath, or chewing on one side can be signs worth showing your vet."
- ✅ "Many vets say mouth health and overall health are connected."
- ❌ "This supplement removes tartar / kills mouth bacteria / prevents gum disease / fixes gut health / slows aging."
- ❌ "Chewing on one side means X disease." Say "can be a sign of pain, ask your vet" instead.
- The oral health → gut health link: present it as "researchers are studying it" or "vets say it's connected", not as settled fact. That this link has strong evidence in dogs is **unverified**.

| Facebook | No official limit found. Hashtags matter little. Use 1 to 3 relevant tags. | **Unverified** best practice | https://www.distribution.ai/blog/facebook-hashtags |

**Suggested sets** (all niche; none appear on any known banned list, but Meta and TikTok do not publish full banned lists, so this is **unverified**):

- **Instagram (5):** `#seniordog #beagle #doghealth #dogdentalcare #dogmomtips`
- **TikTok (5):** `#seniordog #beaglesoftiktok #doghealthtips #dogtok #vetadvice`
  - Note: #vetadvice may imply that Valentina is a vet. Use `#dogcare` instead unless the content is clearly vet-sourced.
- **Facebook (2–3):** `#SeniorDog #DogHealth #Beagle`

**Avoid:** #fyp, #viral, #foryou (no proven benefit, and they take up slots), #ad (not needed once the product is cut), and medical words that imply treatment (#cure, #remedy).

**Should you add #AIgenerated?** Not as a hashtag. It uses 1 of your 5 slots and does not replace the platform labels. Use the **built-in AI labels** instead, plus one plain line in the caption or on screen: *"Valentina is an AI-generated host. Mito is 100% real."* TikTok accepts this kind of caption or on-screen text as a valid disclosure. Meta still requires its own tool.

### First-post timing
- **Sprout Social 2026:** TikTok Tue–Thu 2–6 pm local time. Instagram Tue 1–7 pm, Wed 12–9 pm. Weekends are weakest.

## Do / Don't checklist

**Do**
- Turn on Instagram's **AI-generated profile** label before posting.
- Turn on the per-post **AI label** on IG, FB and TikTok.
- Add one plain line: "Valentina is an AI-generated host. Mito is real."
- Keep the content educational and tell viewers to see a vet.
- Use ≤5 niche hashtags on IG and TikTok, and 1–3 on FB.
- End with a real question, "save this", or "follow for part 2".
- Keep text out of the bottom ~670 px, the top ~270 px and the right ~160 px.

**Don't**
- Don't mention Iron Paws in this video, or anywhere without #ad and the Amazon disclosure.
- Don't let Valentina claim personal use or results ("I give Mito… it worked").
- Don't claim any product treats, cures, prevents or kills anything.
- Don't use "comment YES", "tag 3 friends" or "comment X for the link" on a new account.
- Don't promise comment-to-DM on TikTok. It isn't available in the US, UK or EU.
- Don't hide or deny that Valentina is AI. Don't strip the C2PA metadata.
